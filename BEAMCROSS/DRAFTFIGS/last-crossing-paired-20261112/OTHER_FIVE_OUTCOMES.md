They are supplementary, not the sole inferential presentation for the revised four-outcome analysis.

A useful example of distinct questions is duration: RUN–SED observed change difference is +0.133 s (95% HC3 CI −2.05 to +2.32; raw p=.896), whereas ANCOVA gives −1.63 s (−2.53 to −0.727; p=.00242, original Holm-nine .0218). The group gap already existed at D0 (−2.07 s). ANCOVA estimates a common baseline slope of 0.150 rather than imposing the coefficient 1 implicit in change scores. Thus its adjusted D14 result is not evidence that RUN duration improved more. This conditional result is retained, not promoted over the paired-change question.
