## What changed since the previous commit

The working tree contained a long sequence of exploratory first/last-trial analyses, alternative figures, tail-detector tuning and movie revisions. It also contained a prior cleanup and a tail-code package extraction. All these changes were uncommitted at the start. This cleanup packages the selected final state, preserving the historical evidence externally rather than committing every intermediate.

The new tail mask permits pixels higher than three times the saved centroid height above the beam to contribute, even where the original column-wise no-mouse filter excluded them. Two times centroid height was visually rejected as too low. This restores upright tails extending above the mouse. The original crop and tracked frame mapping remain unchanged. Narrow excluded column runs of at most 15 pixels are restored unless connected to the body-side edge. Original missing-data flags remain explicit.

The deep-swing detector uses five-frame raw-motion smoothing, trial median/MAD normalization, a 3.5 threshold, five-frame closing and minimum event duration, and a peak motion-height cutoff of 10 mm. The former cutoff was 7.5 mm; the user inspected all five additionally accepted events and considered them valid swings. This is exploratory visual validation, not an independent blinded sensitivity/specificity study. Motion height is a motion-weighted median, not static tail anatomy. Motion is not physical momentum.

Whole-tail motion is now vertically unweighted and normalized by geometrical crop area. Its trial mean uses valid locomoting frames only: saved stop flag false and forward speed at least 40 pixels/s. There is no forward-speed division or whole-trace MAD normalization. This replaces the earlier distance-weighted whole-tail score for the current figure.

## Current figure choices

- Baseline group comparisons use each mouse's first retained D0 crossing, not necessarily original trial 1 and not the daily mean.
- Endpoint comparisons use the last retained D14 crossing, not necessarily trial 3. Failed/refused intermediate trials can make the original trial number larger.
- The spatial speed-change panel pairs last D14 with last D0. The scalar D14 panels are endpoint values, not changes or ANCOVA-adjusted means.
- The within-session speed panel shows last minus first separately on D0 and D14. This is a descriptive change across retained trials; fatigue was not measured.
- The pooled event-position histogram still uses its original all-retained D0 selection and excludes slips overlapping stops. The slip-count/score scatters include those overlaps. Do not silently call the histogram first-trial data.
- Tail swings means the sum of accepted event excess areas, not number of swings or average event size. No acquisition-rate division is applied.
- Corrected D0 tail motion p = 0.6121164; tail swings p = 0.7258730. The screenshot's older p = 0.948 / 0.724 belongs to the earlier mask/gate and is superseded.
- Current D0 height p = 0.5581349; D14 tail motion p = 0.80414; D14 swing total p = 0.0167857. These are exploratory omnibus cage/cohort permutation tests. The smallest D14 swing pairwise Holm p is 0.0535714; do not turn the omnibus result into a significant named pair.
- Preserve mouse dots, cage diamonds and mouse-group means; uncertainty on spatial curves is mouse SEM, not cage-model confidence intervals. Retained within-session and paired-analysis reports document sensitivity and cohort effects.
