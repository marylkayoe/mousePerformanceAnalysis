# D0 tail motion and motion-height spatial profiles

Two exploratory profiles, all retained D0 trials (125 crossings, 42 mice). Read canonical rerun row profiles and corrected whole-range score CSVs in place; no tracking or source video decoding. Export script: tests/exportD0TailSpatialProfiles.m; plot/aggregation: plot_profiles.py in this folder.

Height is the median row of the full probability-weighted motion profile, expressed relative to bar top with positive values above. It does not use the score's vertical distance ramp. Apply the movie's centered five-frame mean within contiguous valid segments; do not bridge invalid samples or blank near-bar rows. This describes motion location, not stationary anatomical tail position. Scores are NewWholeNormalized from the corrected whole-range reweighting, unchanged. Both exclude existing tail bad frames and invalid frame pairs. Height additionally requires a finite, nonnegative profile with nonzero total motion. No new video reconstruction was performed.

Spatial convention follows existing tail position profiles: approximate tape pixel centres 42 and 1243, 700 mm apart; camera fraction *1279. Reverse to distance from entry, entry left. Calibration 700/1201 mm/pixel is inherited from one reference recording, not independently measured for each trial. Exclude frames outside tape marks, not entire crossings. Use 10 mm bins. Average finite samples per trial/bin, then equally average available crossings per mouse/bin, then equally average mice per group/bin. Crossings with no eligible samples in a bin do not contribute to that bin. No trial centering or additional spatial smoothing.

Group curves are means with descriptive SEM across mice, not cage-adjusted confidence intervals. Suppress bins with fewer than half the group's mice (6 sedentary, 6 walk, 9 run); no interpolation across suppressed bins. Coverage varies along the beam and is recorded in CSV/JSON. Mouse counts and missingness should accompany interpretation. Score and height validity are recorded separately. No statistical significance or group equivalence is inferred from curve overlap.

The original example image's horizontal scale bar should not be copied without checking its actual spatial mapping. These previews use an explicitly labelled 0–700 mm axis. Data alignment checks enforce equal frame counts, exact original-frame mapping, matching tail flags and timestamps. Tables preserve missing bins and coverage without padding.
